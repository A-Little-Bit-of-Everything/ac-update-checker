# Changelog — Andromeda's Create

## v0.3.2

### "Rails & Rollin' Stock"

**Download:** [Prism/MC Launcher](https://www.andromedacat.net/cdn/Andromeda's%20Create%20v032.zip) · [Modrinth](https://www.andromedacat.net/cdn/Andromeda's%20Create%20v032.mrpack) · [CurseForge](https://www.andromedacat.net/cdn/Andromeda's%20Create%20v032%20CF.zip) · [Server Pack](https://www.andromedacat.net/cdn/Andromeda's%20Create%20Server%20v032.zip)

**Server:** ready-to-run NeoForge server, requires Java 21. **8 GB RAM or higher is recommended** in the launch arguments.

**Minecraft:** 1.21.1 · **NeoForge:** 21.1.262

**10 new mods** were added and **13 were updated**. No mods were removed. This drop leans into **trains and movement** — Steam 'n' Rails, a train navigator, new propulsion, and Create cannon-ball armor — alongside a big round of Create add-on and Sophisticated storage updates.

### Added

**Trains & transit**

- **[Create: Steam 'n' Rails](https://modrinth.com/mod/L3Jv0QZI)** `0.3.0-beta.2` — expands Create's train system with new tracks, cars, and rail infrastructure.
- **[Create Railways Navigator](https://modrinth.com/mod/Dq3STxps)** `0.10.0-C6` — an in-game train route planner and live departure board.
- **[Create Propulsion: Simulated](https://modrinth.com/mod/ApkoHNO9)** `1.1.5` — adds new propulsion and movement options for Create contraptions.

**Create add-ons**

- **[Create: Broken Bad ReBroken](https://modrinth.com/mod/uIfluo9C)** `1.1.3` — adds ore-processing and crushing content for Create.
- **[CBC: Locometal Armor](https://modrinth.com/mod/4qa2vGoU)** `2.0.0` — Create Big Cannons locometal-themed armor set.
- **[Aeronautics: Simulated Copycats](https://modrinth.com/mod/NYBH9vVY)** `1.3.2` — copycat blocks for Create Aeronautics.

**Movement & utility**

- **[Freecam](https://modrinth.com/mod/XeEZ3fK2)** `1.3.0` — detach the camera to fly around and inspect your builds.
- **[Armor Hider](https://modrinth.com/mod/GgG2my3y)** `0.14.5` — hide individual armor pieces from rendering.

**Libraries & frameworks** *(dependencies for the above)*

- **[DragonLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/sbIsGaOV)** `3.0.29` — shared library for MrJulsen's mods (Railways Navigator).
- **[Eunomia](https://modrinth.com/mod/BvzQw4wL)** `0.3.14` — core library used by Armor Hider.
