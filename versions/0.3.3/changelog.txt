# Changelog — Andromeda's Create

## v0.3.3

### "Wings & Wilds"

**Download:** [Prism/MC Launcher](https://www.andromedacat.net/cdn/Andromeda's%20Create%20v033.zip) · [Modrinth](https://www.andromedacat.net/cdn/Andromeda's%20Create%20v033.mrpack) · [CurseForge](https://www.andromedacat.net/cdn/Andromeda's%20Create%20v033%20CF.zip) · [Server Pack](https://www.andromedacat.net/cdn/Andromeda's%20Create%20Server%20v033.zip)

**Server:** ready-to-run NeoForge server, requires Java 21. **8 GB RAM or higher is recommended** in the launch arguments.

**Minecraft:** 1.21.1 · **NeoForge:** 21.1.265

**39 new mods** were added, **24 were updated** (4 removed), and a new custom global resource pack (**Not a Glitch Studios Assets & ReSplash!**) is now pre-bundled alongside **Sparkles** and active by default. This major drop focuses on **world exploration and flight** — introducing **Biomes O' Plenty**, **Arts & Crafts**, **Icarus** wings, **StreamsReflowing**, and automated **RoadArchitect** transit networks alongside a customized **FancyMenu** interface and Create add-ons.

### Resource Packs
- **[Not a Glitch Studios Assets & ReSplash!](https://www.andromedacat.net/resplash/)** &mdash; new custom global resource pack added alongside **Sparkles**, pre-bundled directly via Open Loader (`global_packs/required_resources/`) and active by default. Features over 400 custom splash texts, UI elements credited to **[Vanilla Tweaks](https://vanillatweaks.net/)**, and custom title assets tailored for Andromeda's Create, built upon a customized fork of unilock's **[ReSplash!](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/resplash)**.
- **[Sparkles (Stardust Labs Fork)](https://www.andromedacat.net/sparkles/)** &mdash; pre-bundled in `global_packs/required_resources/` and active by default, ensuring complete texture and localization coverage for Stardust Labs mods (Terralith, Incendium, Nullscape).

### Added — World & Biomes
- **[Biomes O' Plenty](https://modrinth.com/mod/biomes-o-plenty)** `21.1.0.14` — expansive world generation with dozens of new biomes, flora, and building materials.
- **[GlitchCore](https://modrinth.com/mod/glitchcore)** `2.1.0.2` — core library required by Biomes O' Plenty.
- **[Arts & Crafts](https://modrinth.com/mod/artsandcrafts)** `1.5.4` — chalk, plaster, terracotta shingles, and artisan craft decorations.
- **[Arts & Crafts x Biomes O' Plenty](https://modrinth.com/mod/arts-and-crafts-x-biomes-o-plenty)** `1.0.2` — compatibility integration between Arts & Crafts and Biomes O' Plenty.
- **[StreamsReflowing](https://modrinth.com/mod/streams-reflowing)** `2.17.1` — realistic flowing river generation with dynamic currents and scenic riverbanks.

### Added — Flight & Exploration

> **Note:** The following mods ship **disabled by default** (`.disabled` in `mods/`):
> - `Distant Horizons` — render distance / LODs.
> - `AdPother` (Pollution of the Realms) and `smog` (Create: Smog) — optional pollution mechanics.
> - `ambush` — opt-in ambush events.
> - `ticktweak` — optional scheduler optimizations.
> Enable them in the launcher or rename their `.disabled` extension in `mods/`.
